# Step 4: Open it in the browser 🌐

Killercoda can expose a port from your environment as a public URL.

👉 Click here to open the app: [ACCESS APP]({{TRAFFIC_HOST1_30080}})

You'll see the app's response in your browser, including the Pod's hostname. Refresh a few times and you may see it change between the two Pods.

> If the link doesn't work, open the menu (☰, top right) → **Traffic / Ports** → enter `30080`.
